# Working in this repository

- Read `README.md` and `docs/design.md` first. Settled decisions live in
  `docs/decisions/`; change one only by adding a new record that replaces it.
- English in code, comments, commits and docs.
- Conventional commits, short subject. No trailers, and no mention of AI or tools
  in messages or authorship.
- Small pull requests, rebase-merged. Every commit builds and passes the tests.
- Code taken from elsewhere keeps its copyright and license and says where it
  came from. Nothing from GPL projects.
